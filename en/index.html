Here's how the phone breaks

Five steps, zero setup. In less time than it takes to order pizza, you'll have a whole chain of nonsense to show off.

1

Write an impossible word

The weirder and more specific, the better. "Octopus doing yoga" always goes wrong, and that's exactly the point.

2

Someone draws it in 30 seconds

No erasing, no cheating, and no drawing skills required. The time limit does the rest.

3

The next player guesses the drawing

They write down the first thing that comes to mind, no matter how far it strays from the original idea.

4

It repeats until the end

Word, drawing, word, drawing... the chain grows and so does the chaos, round after round.

5

You see the whole chain

Everyone together, start to finish, to discover what monster the original idea turned into.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most before their first match.

Is Telephone Pictionary free?

Yes, the game is free and ad-supported. If you'd rather play without interruptions, you can buy the ad-free Premium version from inside the app.

How many players do you need?

Each match is for 2 to 4 players. You can all play from the same couch or each from your own phone, wherever you are.

Can I play if I don't have friends online?

Yes. The bot mode lets you practice your drawings (and your patience) without depending on anyone else being available.

How long does a match last?

Matches are designed to last under 5 minutes, so they fit into any spare moment: before bed, at dinner, or while waiting for food.

Which devices is it available on?

You can download it on Android through Google Play and on iPhone or iPad through the App Store.

How do the rankings work?

Every point you earn drawing or guessing counts toward three separate leaderboards: weekly, monthly, and yearly, so there's always a new race to win.
